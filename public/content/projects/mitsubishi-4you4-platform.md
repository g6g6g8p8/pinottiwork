When Tech and Soul launched the **4You4** platform for Mitsubishi Motors Brasil, the brief was to anchor the brand around a new kind of driver — more diverse, more connected to the technology inside the car than to the mud outside it. Every campaign that followed had to carry the same DNA: 4x4 spirit, redefined.

Over two years, I helped build and expand that platform across product launches, branded content and a major sports sponsorship — five different expressions of one idea, each built to stand on its own and still read as the same brand.

---

## New Generation — The Platform Launch

The platform's opening statement. The brief was to introduce a Mitsubishi that reflected who was actually driving it: women in leadership, agribusiness entrepreneurs using technology, a more contemporary idea of family. The 4x4 heritage stayed. The audience evolved.

From this campaign onward, every Mitsubishi product in Brazil carried the 4You4 signature.

## 4You4Rally — ESPN Tennis Sponsorship

Mitsubishi sponsored more than 200 tennis matches on ESPN throughout 2021 — Australian Open, US Open, Masters 1000, ATP Finals. Instead of standard media placement, the idea lived in the language itself.

Off-road, Mitsubishi owns _rally_. On a tennis court, a _rally_ is the most intense exchange in the game. **4You4Rally** lived exactly in that overlap.

Three full-3D short films follow a Pajero Sport crossing forest, plantation and beach terrain until it arrives at tennis courts built in the middle of nowhere — collapsing two universes into a single image.

## MIT Rallies — Where 4x4 Lives For Real

If 4You4Rally borrowed tennis's language, this is the original: the off-road events Mitsubishi has run in Brazil for decades, and the reason its 4x4 credentials aren't a marketing claim. Across three films, the platform plugs directly into the community of drivers, families and crews that live the sport every weekend.

**Mitsubishi Cup.** The most traditional single-make cross-country speed rally in Latin America. Pure adrenaline, pro-level competition, and the clearest demonstration of what a Mitsubishi 4x4 can do when the road runs out.

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEU_j5bIJfo "4you4racers")

**Mitsubishi MotorSports.** The regularity rally — same off-road DNA, opened up to family and friends. The 4x4 as a shared adventure instead of a stopwatch.

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3U7iNWmUkKo "4you4adventure")

**Mitsubishi Experience.** The brand experience film that ties the rally circuit back into the 4You4 platform — the events, the people, the terrain, all under one signature.

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O44F4Rhu5dg "4you4experience")

## MIT Drivelines — The 4x4 Travel Companion

A branded content platform that turned the 4x4 DNA into a travel proposition — built with full airline aesthetics. With international travel severely restricted during the pandemic, Mitsubishi launched a fictional travel company called MIT Drivelines, inviting Brazilians to discover the country by car, with the comfort and technology of a premium vehicle standing in for the first-class seat.

"Comissárias" presented curated road-trip routes across Brazil — adventure, culture, food, family — accessible through a dedicated hotsite, where owners also unlocked partner benefits at hotels and restaurants along the routes.

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K_5Q0fRKW5c "MIT Drivelines — Campaign Film")

A creator-led chapter for the platform: photographer Pablo Vaz takes MIT Drivelines into the Serra do Rio do Rastro in Santa Catarina, reaching the kind of landscapes only a Mitsubishi 4x4 can get you to — the travel-company idea proven in the field by someone who actually lives it.

[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ymxmQWYt8e0 "Destination – Rio do Rastro")

Two years, five campaigns, one identity that never had to be reinvented — only re-aimed, from tennis courts to backroads to a fictional airline. That's the real test of a platform: not whether one film works, but whether the fifth one still feels like the first.
