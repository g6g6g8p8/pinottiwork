Next IP has spent 20+ years adapting inside Brazil's communications industry — resilience isn't a value statement there, it's how the company survived.

[video](https://i.imgur.com/pGw735P.mp4 "Concept")

When the marketing team decided it was time to evolve, they brought in Agência Crush to lead a full rebrand. The new visual identity — and the tagline behind it — came out of research with internal teams and external audiences, built around the company's actual purpose: connection.

Once the concept, brand and tagline were signed off, Viela Design Studio joined to bring the new identity to life visually — a rare case of two studios building one brand without either one showing the seams.
